# **AniPals**

## **About the Project**
AniPals is a web-based multiplayer pixel-art farming and life simulation game where players manage an autonomous community of highly skilled animal companions called **AniPals**. Instead of doing all the labor themselves, players strategically assign tasks to their AniPals while expanding, customizing, and optimizing their farm.

The game combines:
- Interactive real-time mini-games
- Idle/offline progression systems
- Social multiplayer interactions
- Competitive and cooperative mechanics
- Gacha-based collectible progression

Players can visit other farms to trade resources, leave gifts, battle other players, or perform friendly crop raids for rewards.

---

## **Core Features**

### **Dynamic Weather System**
Environmental weather conditions cycle periodically and affect gameplay in multiple ways:
- Unique visual screen overlays
- Modified item drop rates
- Exclusive loot availability
- Temporary AniPal efficiency boosts

---

### **Gacha System**
Players can obtain upgraded AniPal variants through a gacha mechanic.

#### **Gacha Mechanics**
- Pull currency is earned through mini-games and gameplay progression
- 5★ AniPal drop rate: **0.006%**
- Guaranteed 5★ AniPal at **75 pulls** (Pity System)
- 5★ AniPals are divided into:
  - Standard
  - Premium
- Players have a **50/50 chance** between Standard and Premium 5★ AniPals
- Losing the 50/50 guarantees the next 5★ AniPal to be Premium
- 4★ AniPals and tools are guaranteed every **10 pulls**
- Separate banners exist for tools and AniPals

---

### **Trading & Social System**
Players can interact using unique player UIDs.

Available interactions include:
- Trade
- Message
- Gift

#### **Trading Features**
- Both players can view all offered items
- Trades require confirmation from both parties
- Trades only proceed once both users accept the confirmation prompt

---

## **Gameplay Flow**

1. User logs into the game
2. User creates an in-game username
3. A live tutorial introduces the gacha system
4. Player receives:
   - Their original AniPal
   - One random starter AniPal
5. After the tutorial, the player is free to progress however they choose

---

## **Gameplay Philosophy**
AniPals is designed to support multiple playstyles:
- Competitive farming optimization
- Casual idle progression
- Social interaction and trading
- Collection-focused gameplay
- Strategic resource management

The game rewards both active players through mini-games and passive players through offline progression systems.
